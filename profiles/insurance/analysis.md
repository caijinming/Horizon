# Evaluation goal

Evaluate the importance of timely developments for readers in China's insurance industry (agents, brokers, insurers, insurtech) and households making protection and savings decisions.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Landmark.** Regulation-level changes now in force (e.g., asset-liability management rules, preset-interest-rate mechanism triggers), industry-wide data releases, or policies affecting tens of millions of households (medical insurance financing, pension reform, long-term-care insurance rollout).
- **7-8: High value.** Major insurer strategy shifts, distribution reforms with clear economic impact (commission rules, bancassurance fees), notable industry claims-data releases, or regional pilots with national scaling signals.
- **5-6: Interesting.** Routine company announcements, single product launches, regional programs, incremental data updates.
- **3-4: Low priority.** Press releases, marketing campaigns, single-incident stories with limited industry significance.
- **0-2: Noise.** Literal non-insurance matches, purely promotional content, or items unrelated to insurance and household finance.

# Evaluation guidance

Prefer items a practitioner could use directly in client communication or compliance-sensitive sales practice. Concrete numbers (rates, payout amounts, effective dates), named regulators, and verifiable aggregate data count as evidence. Popularity on hot-board rankings is not evidence of industry importance; a low-ranked but dated regulatory change outranks a high-ranked celebrity story. Use three to five specific topic tags.
