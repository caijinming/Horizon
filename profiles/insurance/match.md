# Insurance industry profile

Use this profile for timely developments in China's insurance industry and the family-finance and health-security policies that shape it: regulator actions (NFRA, NHSA, MOHRSS, PBoC) including rules, enforcement and penalties; product pricing economics such as preset interest rates, participating and universal products; claims and solvency data releases; medical-insurance, pension and long-term-care policy; distribution channels and agent economics (bancassurance, "bao-xing-he-yi" commission regulation); and major insurer strategic moves.

Typical items include new regulations taking effect, industry-wide data releases, product-generation switches, claims reports from insurers, and policy changes affecting household financial security (deposit rates, childcare subsidies, social insurance).

Do not use this profile for literal non-insurance uses of the word 保险 (保险柜, 保险丝), pure product marketing or sales pitches, single-accident stories without industry significance, or overseas insurance news with no China relevance.
